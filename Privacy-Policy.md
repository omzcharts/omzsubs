# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

At OMZ Charts ("OMZ," "we," "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information related to your use of omzcharts.com, the OMZ Charts Discord community, and any related services. This Privacy Policy describes:

- What information we collect
- How we use it
- How third-party platforms (X, Discord, and any analytics providers we may use) may process data
- Your rights and choices

By using OMZ's services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

OMZ collects minimal information, and only what is necessary to operate our website and services. We may collect:

### 1.1 Website Analytics Data

At this time, omzcharts.com does not use cookies, pixels, analytics scripts, or advertising/retargeting tools. If any such tools are introduced in the future (for example, basic site analytics), this Policy will be updated and, where required, appropriate consent mechanisms will be added.

### 1.2 Communication Data

If you contact OMZ (e.g., through a direct message on X), we may retain the content of that conversation as needed to respond to you. Message data sent through X is also governed by X's own privacy policy.

### 1.3 Social Media Interactions

If you engage with @omzcharts content on X or other platforms (likes, retweets, replies), your data is processed according to those platforms' policies. OMZ does not separately collect personal data from social media interactions.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

OMZ does NOT collect or store:

- Payment information
- Credit card or billing details
- Government-issued identification
- Date of birth
- Addresses
- Social Security numbers

All payments and authentication are handled externally by X's Creator Subscriptions platform.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Integrations

OMZ uses third-party platforms to deliver its services. Each platform has its own privacy policy that governs how it collects and processes data.

### 3.1 X (Twitter) — Payments, Authentication, Subscriptions

X handles:

- Membership sign-ups
- Payment processing
- Billing information
- Subscription renewals
- Account authentication

OMZ does not access, store, or manage any payment information. Please refer to X's Privacy Policy for details.

### 3.2 Discord (Community Access)

Participation in the OMZ Charts Discord means Discord may collect:

- Discord username and user ID
- Messages you send
- Server activity
- IP address
- Device/browser metadata

Your Discord data is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy. OMZ does not download, extract, or sell Discord user data.

### 3.3 Analytics, Cookies & Tracking Tools

OMZ Charts does not currently use cookies, pixels, analytics scripts, conversion-tracking tools, or retargeting tools on omzcharts.com. You may also disable cookies in your browser settings at any time as an additional precaution.

### 3.4 X (Twitter) and Social Media

@omzcharts may share posts, promotions, and educational content on X. OMZ does not collect personal data from your interactions with that account. Your data is governed by X's privacy policies.

## 4. How We Use Data

OMZ uses any information described above to:

- Respond to users who contact us first

- Sell your personal data
- Share identifiable data with advertisers
- Use data to track individual trading behavior

By participating in the OMZ Charts Discord or private feed, you acknowledge that your username, messages, or interactions may be visible in such content. OMZ will make reasonable efforts to remove or obscure personal identifiers upon request.

Our website, Discord community, and services are offered globally but are not tailored to EU/EEA users, currencies, languages, or regulatory frameworks.

If an EU/EEA resident voluntarily accesses OMZ's website or services, they acknowledge and agree that:

- OMZ processes only minimal information necessary to operate its services

OMZ does not use non-essential cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioral profiling targeted at EU/EEA users. If such tools are introduced in the future, OMZ will implement appropriate consent mechanisms where required.

EU/EEA users may message @omzcharts on X to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data voluntarily provided directly to OMZ.
